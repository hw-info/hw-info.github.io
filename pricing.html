How the subscription works

A subscription buys a maintenance period of 1, 2 or 3 years. During that period you can license every Pro version that is released. When it ends, the versions released during the period keep working as Pro; only the right to license newer versions lapses.

Renewal is cheaper than the first purchase. The licence page gives a renewal discount of 50% to 60%, with renewals from $14.50. If you let it lapse you are not locked out of the program, since the free edition and your last licensed Pro version both remain usable. What you lose by staying on the free edition is set out on the free vs Pro page.

The lifetime licence

The lifetime licence is a one time purchase that covers all future updates of HWiNFO 64 and ARM64 Pro. The licence page shows it from $199. The page text available for this guide does not make clear which tier that figure belongs to, so confirm the amount for your tier at checkout.

As a rough guide using the from prices only, a first year at $29 followed by renewals at $14.50 stays below $199 for more than a decade. The author describes lifetime as the premium option and points price sensitive buyers to the subscription.

Refunds and payment

Refunds are accepted up to 14 days from the date of the order and are subject to individual review, according to the licence page and the licence agreement. That is a review, not an unconditional money back guarantee.

Payment runs through 2Checkout (Verifone), which bills you and emails the licence key. The vendor points to 2Checkout's own refund policy for processing details. No coupon codes are published on the vendor pages read for this guide; the only standing discounts are the renewal and volume discounts.

Which licence to choose

  • Home PC, occasional checks: stay on the free edition from the download page.
  • Home PC with a dashboard, gadget or more than 5 on screen display items: Pro Personal subscription.
  • Technician who works on many machines: Pro Engineer, licensed per user.
  • Company machines with shared users: Pro Corporate, licensed per computer.
  • Remote monitoring: the licence on the central system has to cover all connected machines.

Frequently asked questions

How much does HWiNFO Pro cost?

A subscription starts from $29 for the first year and renews from $14.50. A lifetime licence starts from $199. Local taxes are added at checkout, where the final price for your country is shown.

Is there a HWiNFO lifetime licence?

Yes. The lifetime licence is a one time purchase that covers all future updates of HWiNFO 64 and ARM64 Pro. The licence page shows it from $199.

What is the HWiNFO refund policy?

Refunds are accepted up to 14 days from the date of the order and each request is reviewed individually. Payments and refunds are processed by 2Checkout (Verifone).

What happens when my subscription ends?

Pro versions released during your maintenance period keep working. You lose only the right to license versions released afterwards, until you renew.

Do I need a licence to use HWiNFO at work?

Yes for HWiNFO64 and the ARM64 build: commercial use requires an Engineer or Corporate Pro licence, and the Personal tier does not cover it. HWiNFO32 and the DOS build are freeware that may be used commercially.

Is there a HWiNFO coupon or discount?

The vendor pages read for this guide list no coupon codes. The published discounts are 50% to 60% on renewals and a volume discount on all three Pro tiers.
