Licence terms in short

HWiNFO64 and HWiNFO ARM64 are freeware for non commercial use only. HWiNFO32 and the DOS build are freeware and may also be used commercially. Using the 64 bit or ARM64 build at work requires a Pro licence, and the Personal tier of Pro does not cover commercial use either.

Pro subscriptions start from $29 for the first year and a lifetime licence starts from $199, before local taxes. Refunds are accepted up to 14 days from the order date, subject to individual review. The full table is on the HWiNFO Pro price page, and the review helps you decide whether you need Pro at all.

Frequently asked questions

Is the HWiNFO download safe?

Take it from hwinfo.com, the vendor's own site, which is where the button on this page leads. The vendor states the program does not collect personal information or transmit data over the network. Avoid mirror sites and repackaged copies.

Should I pick HWiNFO64 or HWiNFO32?

Pick HWiNFO64 on any 64 bit Windows from Windows 7 onward; it is what the installer sets up. HWiNFO32 is the legacy build in the portable package for 32 bit systems back to Windows 95.

Is there a trial of HWiNFO Pro?

The vendor pages read for this guide do not describe a Pro trial. The free edition is the way to try the program, and Pro purchases have a 14 day refund window that is subject to individual review.

Can I test a beta without reinstalling?

Yes. Betas come in portable format only, and the author's advice is to copy the file from the beta package over the one in the installed folder.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is mentioned for the free download. A Pro licence is bought through 2Checkout (Verifone), which emails the licence key after payment.
